Seven disciplines, covering the full lifecycle of your site infrastructure.

From initial design through supply, installation and commissioning — each line below can be scoped on its own or combined into a single project.

Transformer installations

Installation, testing and commissioning of power and distribution transformers for industrial, commercial and utility clients — including foundations, bunding, cable termination and protection tie-ins so the unit is ready to energise on handover.

  • Distribution transformers
  • Power transformers
  • Testing & commissioning
  • Cable termination

Switchgear installation

Installation of HV and LV switchgear, distribution panels and protection systems, wired and set up to isolate faults and protect the equipment and people downstream of them.

  • HV / LV switchgear
  • Distribution panels
  • Protection systems

Instrumentation

Installation, calibration and maintenance of measurement and control instrumentation, so the people running a process can see and trust what it's actually doing.

  • Measurement & control
  • Calibration
  • Process instrumentation

Solar installations and design

System design and installation of solar PV systems — sized against the site's real load profile, then installed and commissioned, from panel mounting through to inverter and battery integration.

  • System design
  • PV installation
  • Inverter & battery integration

Metal fabrication

Custom fabrication of steel structures, brackets, enclosures and support steelwork — built to support transformers, panels, solar arrays and other equipment on site.

  • Steel structures
  • Enclosures
  • Support steelwork

Electrical construction

Complete electrical construction services including wiring and cabling installation, power distribution systems, conduit work, control systems and automation, perimeter security fencing, and safety systems such as earthing and grounding — all engineered and installed to specification for industrial, commercial and residential sites.

  • Perimeter security fencing
  • Electrical wiring & cabling
  • Power distribution
  • Control systems & automation
  • Safety systems (earthing & grounding)
  • Cable termination

Supply of electrical equipment and components

Sourcing and supply of transformers, switchgear, cabling and electrical components — for contractors and site owners who need equipment specified and supplied to standard.

  • Transformers & switchgear
  • Cabling
  • Components & spares
