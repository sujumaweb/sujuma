About Sujuma

Engineering the power and site infrastructure Zambian sites depend on.

Sujuma Engineering Limited works across seven core disciplines that cover a site's electrical and structural needs from one contractor: transformer and switchgear installation, instrumentation, solar design and installation, metal fabrication, electrical perimeter fencing, and the supply of electrical equipment and components.

Whether the brief is a new transformer bay, a solar system for a commercial site, or fabricated steelwork to support it all, projects are scoped, engineered and installed by the same team — so specification, safety and workmanship stay consistent from first assessment to final commissioning.

Integrity

Straight scoping and honest reporting on every site.

Quality assurance

Work tested and commissioned against the original spec.

Accountability

One contractor, one team, from design through handover.

How it runs

From site assessment to handover.

Every project — a transformer bay or a full solar install — moves through the same four stages, so nothing gets specified or installed without a clear line back to the original brief.

01

Assess

Survey the site, existing infrastructure, load requirements and constraints before anything is specified.

02

Design

Engineer the solution and equipment specification against the brief, site conditions and applicable standards.

03

Install

Install to spec, with qualified technicians working to safe systems of work on site.

04

Commission

Test, commission and hand over with documentation the client can act on and maintain against.
